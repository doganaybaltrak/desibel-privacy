# desibel Autopilot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

desibel Autopilot is a personal tool operated by Doganay Baltrak (desibel). It helps the operator create, schedule and publish his own Pins on his own Pinterest business account and view the performance of those Pins. It is used only by its operator and is not offered to the public.

## What data the app accesses

The app accesses only the operator's own Pinterest boards and Pins (to create Pins and choose a board), analytics for the operator's own Pins (impressions, saves, clicks), and the operator's own Canva designs (to generate Pin images from his templates). It does not access other users' Pins, boards, messages or personal data.

## Where data is stored

Everything is stored locally on the operator's own computer (a local database and files). Access tokens are kept in a local file that is not shared. Nothing is uploaded to any server run by desibel, and no data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

## How data is used

Data is used only to draft, schedule and publish the operator's own content and to show him performance reports. Pins are published only after the operator approves them.

## Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept only as long as the app is used. It can be deleted at any time by deleting the local files. Access can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest and Canva account settings.

## Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone other than its operator.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
